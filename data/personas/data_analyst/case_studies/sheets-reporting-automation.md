# Google Sheets Reporting Automation

**Client industry:** Agency  
**Completed:** 2022-03  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: account managers pasted numbers into client reports every Friday. They described what they wanted as a self-updating team spreadsheet.

## What I built

It relied on shared sheets with import formulas and protected ranges.
It relied on triggers that mail a weekly summary from a sheet.
Excel did the heavy lifting here.

## Outcome

Result: saved 6 hours a week per manager; zero copy-paste errors afterwards.
