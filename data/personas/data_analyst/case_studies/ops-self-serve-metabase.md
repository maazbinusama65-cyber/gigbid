# Self-serve Metabase for Ops

**Client industry:** Logistics  
**Completed:** 2023-06  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: ops staff queued analyst requests for simple counts. They described what they wanted as a question builder for non-technical staff.

## What I built

Metabase did the heavy lifting here.
It relied on reporting queries over millions of rows.
It relied on SQL tables with constraints, not loose files.
On the engineering side I put in place targets versus actuals with traffic-light status.

## Outcome

Result: analyst request backlog down 60%.
