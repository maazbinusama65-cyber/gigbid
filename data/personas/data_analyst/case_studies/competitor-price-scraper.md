# Competitor Price Scraper

**Client industry:** E-commerce  
**Completed:** 2023-05  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: pricing staff checked competitor sites by hand. They described what they wanted as a market price monitor.

## What I built

We ended up with daily price snapshots pulled from competitor catalogues.
I set up a set of small automation tools.
We ended up with scripted reshaping of large spreadsheets into tidy tables.
I used PostgreSQL for this part.

## Outcome

Result: 12k prices tracked daily; price-match response time down from days to hours.
