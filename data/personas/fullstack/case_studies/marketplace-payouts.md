# Marketplace Payouts

**Client industry:** Online marketplace  
**Completed:** 2023-03  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: sellers were paid manually every Friday. They described what they wanted as a split payments for sellers.

## What I built

Stripe did the heavy lifting here.
Under the hood: Express.
Under the hood: PostgreSQL.
TypeScript did the heavy lifting here.

## Outcome

Result: automatic payouts with reconciliation; finance saved a day a week.

## Stack

Stripe, Express, PostgreSQL, TypeScript
