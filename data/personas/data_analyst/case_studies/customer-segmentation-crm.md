# Customer Segmentation

**Client industry:** Retail  
**Completed:** 2022-06  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: the CRM sent one newsletter to everybody. They described what they wanted as a spender groups for CRM.

## What I built

We ended up with buyers grouped by behaviour for targeted campaigns.
I set up scripts and services in a typed, tested codebase.
I used pandas for this part.
Under the hood: Power BI.

## Outcome

Result: 5 segments; targeted emails got 31% higher click rate.

## Stack

pandas, Power BI
