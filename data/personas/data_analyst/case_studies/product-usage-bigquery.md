# Product Usage Analytics on BigQuery

**Client industry:** SaaS  
**Completed:** 2024-04  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: the product team had raw events but no adoption numbers. They described what they wanted as a feature adoption tracking.

## What I built

We ended up with a serverless warehouse queried over billions of event rows.
It relied on reporting queries over millions of rows.
Looker Studio did the heavy lifting here.
We ended up with inconsistent names, dates and codes normalised.

## Outcome

Result: 20 event tables modelled; feature adoption dashboards guided two roadmap cuts.
