# SaaS Subscription Billing Portal

**Client industry:** SaaS  
**Completed:** 2023-02  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: customers emailed support to change plans and get invoices. They described what they wanted as a self-serve plan upgrades.

## What I built

A key piece was server-rendered React pages with route-level data fetching.
I used TypeScript for this part.
The stack included Stripe.
On the engineering side I put in place SQL tables with constraints, not loose files.
We ended up with migrations and queries driven from one declarative schema.
We ended up with utility-first styling with a shared design-token config.

## Outcome

Result: support tickets about billing dropped 64% and upgrades rose 12%.

## Stack

TypeScript, Stripe
