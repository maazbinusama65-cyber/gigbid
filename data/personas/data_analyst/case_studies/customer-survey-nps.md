# Customer Survey and NPS Analysis

**Client industry:** Hospitality  
**Completed:** 2022-11  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: 2,300 survey responses sat unread in a spreadsheet. They described what they wanted as a satisfaction study.

## What I built

Under the hood: survey analysis.
We ended up with scripted statistical analysis with tidy data verbs.
A key piece was careful inference instead of eyeballing charts.
ggplot2 did the heavy lifting here.

## Outcome

Result: five complaint themes quantified; one fix lifted NPS 8 points.

## Stack

survey analysis, ggplot2
