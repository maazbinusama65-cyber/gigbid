# Dynamic Pricing Experiments

**Client industry:** Travel  
**Completed:** 2022-09  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: discounts were set by gut feel. They described what they wanted as a price test engine.

## What I built

We ended up with split tests read out with confidence intervals.
I set up a tabular baseline with preprocessing and grid search.
It relied on tabular wrangling in dataframes with merges and group-bys.
We ended up with reporting queries over millions of rows.

## Outcome

Result: found a 7% price band with +4.1% revenue and no conversion loss.
