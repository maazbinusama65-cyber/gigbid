# Streamlit Pricing Explorer

**Client industry:** Retail  
**Completed:** 2024-06  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: pricing scenarios lived in one person's laptop spreadsheet. They described what they wanted as a interactive what-if pricing page.

## What I built

Streamlit did the heavy lifting here.
This is where Python came in.
A key piece was tabular wrangling in dataframes with merges and group-bys.
On the engineering side I put in place next-quarter numbers with seasonality and prediction ranges.

## Outcome

Result: category managers run their own scenarios; adopted by 8 teams.

## Stack

Streamlit, Python
