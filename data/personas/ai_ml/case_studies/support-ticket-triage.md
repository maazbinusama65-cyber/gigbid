# Support Ticket Triage Classifier

**Client industry:** SaaS  
**Completed:** 2023-03  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: support agents spent the first hour of each shift sorting tickets by hand. They described what they wanted as a inbox routing assistant.

## What I built

The stack included Python.
On the engineering side I put in place a pretrained language model fine-tuned from a public model hub.
NLP did the heavy lifting here.
FastAPI did the heavy lifting here.
We ended up with a reproducible image with a one-command local start.
The stack included MLflow.

## Outcome

Result: first-response time fell 38% and mis-routed tickets dropped from 21% to 6%.
