# Content Platform on Supabase

**Client industry:** Media  
**Completed:** 2024-07  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: editors waited on developers to publish. They described what they wanted as a editor-friendly publishing site.

## What I built

A key piece was a backend-as-a-service for auth, storage and database.
We ended up with a site with file-based routing and incremental regeneration.
We ended up with shared type definitions between front end and back end.
Under the hood: Tailwind CSS.

## Outcome

Result: editors publish without developers; 150 articles migrated.
