# Subscriber Cohort Retention Analysis

**Client industry:** Digital media  
**Completed:** 2022-10  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: leadership saw a flat churn number that hid very different cohorts. They described what they wanted as a who sticks around study.

## What I built

This is where cohort analysis came in.
On the engineering side I put in place hand-written analytical queries with window functions.
A key piece was a set of small automation tools.
pandas did the heavy lifting here.
On the engineering side I put in place significance tests, confidence intervals and effect sizes.

## Outcome

Result: found a 3x gap between acquisition channels, redirecting 15% of ad spend.

## Stack

cohort analysis, pandas
