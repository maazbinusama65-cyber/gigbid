# Review Sentiment Monitor

**Client industry:** Consumer apps  
**Completed:** 2022-02  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: product managers read reviews by hand once a quarter. They described what they wanted as a customer voice tracker.

## What I built

A key piece was text understanding for messy free-form customer messages.
On the engineering side I put in place a pretrained language model fine-tuned from a public model hub.
I set up reporting queries over millions of rows.
I set up a set of small automation tools.

## Outcome

Result: a weekly digest of themes; an issue was caught 9 days earlier than before.
