# Marketing Attribution Model

**Client industry:** E-commerce  
**Completed:** 2023-07  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: last-click reporting overcredited branded search. They described what they wanted as a which campaign earned the sale.

## What I built

The stack included SQL.
I set up partitioned cloud tables keeping scan costs low.
I set up a link-shared report that refreshes itself.
The stack included statistics.
On the engineering side I put in place scripts and services in a typed, tested codebase.

## Outcome

Result: reallocated 20% of budget; blended CAC down 14%.
