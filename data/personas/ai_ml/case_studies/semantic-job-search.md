# Semantic Job Search

**Client industry:** HR tech  
**Completed:** 2022-05  
**Role:** sole engineer, from first call to hand-over

## The problem

The client had a familiar pain: keyword search hid strong candidates who used different words. They described what they wanted as a meaning-based candidate matching.

## What I built

I set up meaning-based similarity instead of keyword matching.
On the engineering side I put in place a hosted similarity-search store with metadata filters.
The stack included NLP.
It relied on a typed async HTTP service with auto-generated docs.

## Outcome

Result: recruiters shortlisted 2.3x faster and found 18% more qualified matches.
